About Boss Town Tycoon

Boss Town Tycoon is an idle business game. You open shops, they make money on their own, and you spend that money on more shops. A quiet street turns into a busy town, and later grows past airlines and rockets into a Moon Base, a Mars Colony and a Galaxy Network, then over the rainbow to the Dream Worlds: a Rainbow Railway, a Dino Time Park, Candy Planet, Dragon Delivery and a Wish Star Factory.

It suits short visits: the game keeps earning while you are away (up to 8 hours). Managers, upgrades, Rush Hour, missions, Boss Ranks, investors and Board Room perks keep making you richer, and Mayor Mo pops up with a tip whenever something new unlocks.

3 quick tips

  1. Hire a manager for each shop as soon as you can. A shop without a manager stops after every sale and earns nothing while you are away.
  2. Aim for the milestone bars. Pushing an old shop from 90 to 100 often gives more than one new expensive shop.
  3. Claim missions as they finish. Stars are permanent, and the rank bonus applies to every future town.
